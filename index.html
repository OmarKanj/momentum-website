⚠️ Important Setup Information

macOS Users: Since Momentum is not code-signed, you'll need to remove the quarantine attribute after downloading:

Open Terminal and run: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Momentum.app

Windows Users: You may see a SmartScreen warning. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway" to proceed.

Linux Users: You may need to make the file executable with: chmod +x momentum
